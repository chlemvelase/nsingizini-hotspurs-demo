More than a football club

A club with
a purpose.

Nsingizini Hotspurs represents the spirit, pride and ambition of Shiselweni. From its roots in the community to competing at the highest level of Eswatini football, the club continues to build its story through hard work, belief and togetherness.

Known to supporters as Insingizi Yezulu, the club has become a force in domestic football and a source of pride for its supporters.
