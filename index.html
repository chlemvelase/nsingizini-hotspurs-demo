More than a football club

A community.
A legacy.

Nsingizini Hotspurs began in 1984 as a community football initiative in Nsingizini, near Hluthi in the Shiselweni Region. Four decades on, it is a force in the MTN Premier League and a proud representative of Eswatini in CAF competitions.

Known to supporters as Insingizi Yezulu and Umgugudla Lomkhulu, the club wears green and white with the pride of an entire region.
